# Code of Conduct

We want the sealsbroken community to be welcoming, respectful, and useful to
people with different backgrounds, abilities, and levels of experience.
Everyone participating is expected to follow this code, including maintainers.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with patience and respect. Make room for beginners and different
  perspectives, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, nationality, religion,
  gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, or other personal traits.
- Discuss ideas and changes on their merits. Give specific, constructive feedback
  and accept corrections without personal attacks.
- Respect people's time and boundaries. Keep discussions relevant and avoid
  repeatedly contacting someone who has asked you to stop.
- Protect privacy. Get permission before sharing someone else's personal details,
  private messages, images, or recordings.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexual harassment or unwanted
sexual attention, personal insults, and deliberately hostile disruption are not
acceptable. Neither are publishing private information without permission,
retaliating against someone who reports a concern, or evading moderation.

## Scope

This code applies to sealsbroken repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, and other organization-managed community spaces. It also applies
when someone officially represents the organization in another space.

These are the organization's default standards. A repository may publish more
specific community rules.

## Reporting concerns

Report conduct concerns privately to [lead@onym.app](mailto:lead@onym.app).
Include the relevant repository or community space, what happened, and links or
other context you are comfortable sharing. Do not post sensitive details in a
public issue.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Information may need to be
shared with maintainers responsible for investigating or taking action. If a
report concerns the person reviewing it, ask for another maintainer to review
it where one is available.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may edit or remove content, end a discussion, issue a warning,
restrict participation temporarily, or remove someone from community spaces.
The response should reflect the seriousness, impact, and pattern of behavior;
serious incidents may require immediate restrictions.

Maintainers should explain decisions to the people affected when practical,
without exposing private information. If you believe a decision was mistaken,
contact [lead@onym.app](mailto:lead@onym.app) with the context you want reviewed.
